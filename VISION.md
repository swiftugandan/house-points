# Vision

House Points is a family bank that lives on parents' phones. Children aged roughly 5 to 14 earn points for chores and for behaviour that reflects their family's values. Points grow with interest while they are left alone, and a child can cash them out for real money whenever they choose. The purpose is to give children an early, concrete, repeated experience that work earns money, that waiting makes it grow, and that the record is fair and open to them. Any family can use it with any number of children, without accounts, servers or an internet connection. Parents' phones keep each other up to date over the home Wi-Fi, without the internet. **Done means:** a family can set up the app on two parents' phones and run their children's points for a full school term without an internet connection. During that time every balance shown matches on both phones after each sync, every point can be traced to a recorded reason, and each child can see, in a form suited to their age, what they have, why they have it, and what waiting will earn them.
